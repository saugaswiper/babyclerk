# Feedback

What goes here: **feedback from you, classmates, and testers** — what worked, what confused, what's missing, feature requests, bug reports from real use.

Why it matters: this is ground truth on whether the app actually makes clerkship a breeze ([[Mission]]). It's the highest-signal input for [[Backlog]] priorities — real friction beats guessed friction.

For each: who said it, what they were trying to do, and the underlying need (not just the requested fix). Add via [[_Intake-Template]], list in [[Index]].
