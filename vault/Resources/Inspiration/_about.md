# Inspiration

What goes here: **apps, products, features, and UX that match the vision** — Anki, UWorld, AMBOSS, Duolingo, or anything whose flow, feel, or feature you want to borrow. Screenshots, links, teardowns.

Why it matters: this is the fastest way to communicate design intent to the coding agent — "make the review flow feel like *this*" beats a paragraph of description. It shapes design decisions and the [[Roadmap]].

For each: capture *what specifically* you like (the spaced-rep animation? the confidence buttons? the exam-readiness dashboard?) and *what to avoid*. Add via [[_Intake-Template]], list in [[Index]], drop screenshots in `../_assets/`.
