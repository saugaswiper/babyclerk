# Clinical-References

What goes here: **guidelines, high-yield summaries, algorithms, drug tables** — the authoritative sources that keep content accurate and current.

Why it matters: [[Principles]] demands evidence-first content — a wrong fact is worse than a missing one. These are what AI-generated and original cards get checked against, and what "traceable" means in practice.

Add one via [[_Intake-Template]] and list it in [[Index]]. Note the publication date — guidelines age. Drop source files in `../_assets/`.
