# Content-Sources

What goes here: **decks, question banks, and notes you want to model, import, or draw from** — Anki exports, your own notes, sample cards, question formats you like.

⚠️ **Licensing first.** Before anything from here gets *bundled into the public app*, it must clear [[Licensing-and-Copyright]]. Your own content and openly-licensed material can ship; commercial/third-party material is **import-only, private** (see [[Content-Strategy]]). Set the `license` field in every intake note.

Why it matters: this is the raw material for the original core and for modeling card formats. It shapes [[Content-Strategy]] and the deck/import features.

Add one via [[_Intake-Template]] and list it in [[Index]]. Drop source files in `../_assets/`.
