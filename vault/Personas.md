# Personas

Built **Queen's-first, others welcome** (see [[Vision]]). The primary persona anchors decisions; the others keep us honest about generalizing.

## P1 — Priya, the Queen's clerk (primary)
- MD clerkship student rotating through Medicine, Surgery, OB/GYN, Peds, Psych, Family, Emerg.
- Studies in 15–30 min bursts: on transit, between patients, before bed. Often poor signal in the hospital.
- Overwhelmed by *choice*: UWorld, Anki decks (Montis, etc.), lecture notes, rotation guides. Doesn't know what to prioritize today.
- Has exams at the **end of each rotation** (dates known in advance) and MCCQE looming.
- **Needs:** "Tell me what to study now, don't let me forget the high-yield stuff, and get me ready for each rotation exam."
- **Wins with:** zero-friction daily loop, offline PWA, weak-area targeting, exam-date awareness.

## P2 — Marcus, the classmate (early adopter)
- Priya's cohort. Adopts what Priya recommends. Wants his *own* progress, private.
- Validates: [[Sync-and-Accounts|accounts]] scale cleanly; each user's data is isolated (RLS); onboarding is trivial.

## P3 — Sam, the out-of-school student (the "others welcome" test)
- Med student at a different school / country. Curriculum differs; rotation names and exam styles vary.
- **Needs:** the engine and original content to be useful without Queen's-specific assumptions; ability to import their own school's content.
- Keeps us disciplined: no school hardcoding in the core (see [[Principles]]); content is layered so a school profile is data, not code.

## P4 — The pre-exam crammer (a mode, not a person)
- Any user in the 1–2 weeks before a rotation exam.
- **Needs:** intensity ramp, weak-area blitz, "what to expect on this exam," high-yield review over new material.
- Drives [[AI-Personalization-Engine|date-aware prioritization]].

## Anti-persona (who we don't optimize for)
- Pre-clinical students wanting a full basic-science curriculum (we're clerkship-focused).
- Attendings/residents wanting a point-of-care reference (see [[Vision]] non-goals).
